both ends, once upright and once upside down. That keeps the back identical
after a half turn, so it never reveals a reversed card.

## Card faces: public domain

The 78 card images in `cards/rws1909/` are Pamela Colman Smith's art from the
1909 "Roses & Lilies" printing of the Rider–Waite–Smith deck. They are in the
public domain. Aeonic Arts claims no copyright in them. Sources:
`cards/rws1909/sources.json`.

## Golden Dawn attributions: public domain source

`cards/golden-dawn-book-t.json` is taken from Book T (Hermetic Order of the
Golden Dawn, c. 1888; printed 1912), which is in the public domain.
