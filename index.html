Intuitive Shuffle

Shuffle while you hold your question. Stop when you feel the ding.

Three piles. Each pile stops at an Ace or after 13 cards. An upright Ace is a yes, a reversed Ace is a no. Reversed cards are always on for this spread.

How to shuffle
Let the deck feel your hand (motion)

Cards jump out rarely and at random. Movement, uneven taps on the table, a faster shuffle and a longer wait make a jumper more likely, but never certain. In swipe mode cards can only jump while you swipe, and faster or more uneven swipes raise the chance. Every card always has the same chance.
